### Full description (2310/4000)
```
CSE Companion is a study planner, focus timer and question bank for people preparing for competitive civil services exams. It works offline, keeps your data on your phone, and has no ads.

PLAN
• Choose your subjects and exam dates and get a study plan that follows your progress. Finish a subject and the next one unlocks.
• See every day, week, month and year of your plan. Each day is split into study sessions you can reschedule at any time.
• A clear "on track" view shows when you are likely to finish.

FOCUS
• A stopwatch and a Pomodoro timer with calming live backgrounds such as aurora, night rain, moonlit sea and fireflies.
• Optional ambient sounds to help you concentrate.
• Save your study time against a planned session, or just add it to today's total.
• Streaks, a study calendar and daily totals keep you consistent.

PRACTISE WITH PAST QUESTIONS
• Browse previous-year questions filed by exam and year, including many optional subject papers.
• See which topics were asked most often, ranked by years asked or by number of questions, so you can decide what to study first.
• Track chapters, revision and mock-test scores in one place.

YOURS, ON EVERY PHONE
• Works fully offline after the first open.
• Optional sign-in with your Google account keeps a private copy of your plan, progress and settings and updates your other phones automatically. Without signing in, nothing leaves your phone.
• Choose from many app themes and timer backgrounds. Your choices follow you across phones.
• Download a backup file at any time. You can delete your online data and account from inside the app.

FREE TRIAL AND SUBSCRIPTION
Every feature is free for 14 days, with nothing charged. After that, a yearly subscription is needed to keep using the app. The price, any first-year offer and the renewal price are shown in the app and on the Google Play payment screen before you pay. The subscription renews every year until you cancel it in Google Play (Menu > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

YOUR PRIVACY
We do not sell your data or show ads. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off unless you say yes. Read our privacy policy at csecompanion.in/privacy.html.

IMPORTANT
CSE Companion is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Union Public Service Commission or any government body. Always check official websites for notifications, syllabus and exam rules. Questions in the bank are provided as a study reference; some were read from scanned papers and may contain small mistakes, so please check against the official paper when it matters. The app cannot predict exam results.

Questions or feedback? Write to support@csecompanion.in.
```
