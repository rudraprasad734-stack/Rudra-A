# UPSC PYQ question bank (source for the Civil Service Dossier page)

Exact question text transcribed from the question papers in the Google Drive folder "Upsc question bank".

- `transcripts/mains/` — Mains GS I–IV and Essay 2014–2023, GS I–IV 2024–2026, Anthropology Papers I and II 2023–2026. Line format: `Q1(a)|M10|question text`, with continuation lines below.
- `transcripts/prelims/` — Prelims transcriptions checked against the scans. Line format: `Q1|text`, then `a|`…`d|` options; `P|passage` sets a passage.
- `prelims-drafts-unverified/` — machine OCR drafts of the remaining Prelims scans (2014 CSAT, 2015–2019 GS I and CSAT). **Not proofread yet — do not publish as-is.**
- `bank-docs/` — built JSON documents loaded into the page's `pyq` collection (`scripts/build.py`).
- `page/index.html` — page source.
- Not readable (image-only scans over 20 MB): Prelims 2022 GS I and CSAT, 2024 CSAT, 2025 CSAT, 2026 GS I and CSAT.
