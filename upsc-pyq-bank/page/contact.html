What we sell and how it is delivered

CSE Companion is a digital study service: a study planner, a focus timer and a previous-year question bank, used on a phone or computer. Everything is free for 14 days. After that, access costs ₹299 for one year when paid on the website (one payment, no automatic renewal), or a yearly subscription through Google Play in the Android app.

Delivery. There is nothing to ship. Access is added to your account within a minute of a successful payment and works on every phone and computer where you sign in with the same Google account. No physical goods are sold.

Payments. Website payments are processed by Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking). We never see your card or bank details. See the Terms, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.
