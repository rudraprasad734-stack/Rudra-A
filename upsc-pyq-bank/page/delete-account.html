Delete your account

CSE Companion · Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short

  • You can delete your online copy and your account yourself, inside the app, in under a minute.
  • If you cannot open the app, email us and we will delete it for you within 30 days.
  • Without signing in, we hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open CSE Companion and sign in with the Google account you used.
  2. Go to Settings → Cloud sync.
  3. Tap Delete my cloud copy and account and confirm.

This removes the study data we hold online for your account and deletes the account itself. If Google asks you to sign in again for security, do so and the deletion will finish.

Option 2: ask us by email

Send an email from the address you signed in with to support@csecompanion.in with the subject Delete my account. We may reply to confirm it is you. We will delete your online data and account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

DataWhat happens
Google account name, email, photo and account ID held by our sign-in serviceDeleted with your account
Synced study data (plan, sessions, notes, scores, settings) in your online copyDeleted
Data stored on your own phoneNot touched — clear the app’s data or uninstall to remove it. Download a backup first if you want to keep it.
Anonymous usage statistics (only if you agreed)Not linked to your name or email; removed automatically by our analytics provider after up to 2 months

Deletion cannot be undone. Make a backup with Download backup in the app before you delete if you may want your data later.

If you have a subscription

Your CSE Companion subscription is billed by Google Play, not by us, so deleting your account or data does not cancel it. To stop renewals, open Google Play → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CSE Companion → Cancel, or use Manage subscription in the app’s Settings. You can delete your data with or without a subscription, and in any order.

Questions

Write to support@csecompanion.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
