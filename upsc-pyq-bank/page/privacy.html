Privacy Policy

UPSC Companion · Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short

  • Without signing in, everything you enter stays on your own phone. We never receive it.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, a private copy of your study data is stored in your own account so you can use it on other phones and restore it if you lose your phone.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone to market to you.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off unless you say yes. They never include your name, email or study content.
  • You can delete your online copy and your account at any time, from inside the app.

1. Who we are

UPSC Companion is a study planner, timer and question bank for UPSC Civil Services aspirants. It is run by Rudra Prasad Sharma (“we”, “us”), Yangang, Namchi district, Sikkim, India. Contact: rudraprasad734@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app works fully offline. These items are saved in your phone’s browser or app storage and are not sent to us unless you sign in (see section 3):

  • Your profile: name, district, state and the title you choose (for example “IAS”).
  • Your study plan, chapters completed, study sessions, notes, timer and Pomodoro history, mock-test scores, revision ticks, streaks and reminders settings.
  • Your app settings, such as the colour theme.

Reminders are created by your own phone. They do not go through our servers.

3. If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you do, we receive from Google your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID. We use these only to recognise your account and to show you which account is signed in.

The app also uploads the study data listed in section 2 (except your reminder settings) to a private area that only your account can read and write. We use it to:

  • keep your phone and any other phone you use in step,
  • let you restore your plan and progress after losing or changing your phone, and
  • if we offer paid plans, check whether your plan is active.

We do not read your study data for any other purpose and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

4. Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

The first time you use the app, we ask whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. It is off unless you say yes, and you can turn it on or off at any time in Settings → Anonymous usage statistics. If you say no, no statistics are sent.

If you say yes, the app sends short event records using Google Analytics for Firebase. They tell us things like:

  • which screens are opened and which features are used (for example, a timer was started, a plan was created, a chapter was marked complete, a theme was changed);
  • the type and size of your phone, its operating system and language, and the app version;
  • your approximate location (country, state or city), worked out by Google from your internet address, which is not kept in full; and
  • a random installation ID, stored on your phone, so repeat visits can be counted. It is not linked to your name or email.

We never send your name, email, profile details, notes, subjects, chapter names, plan, scores or anything you type. We have switched off Google’s advertising features and ad personalisation for this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

We use these figures only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Google keeps this data for up to 2 months. We can see only totals and trends, not who you are.

Data-sharing settings in Google Analytics. We have left three of Google’s optional data-sharing settings switched on:

  • Benchmarking: anonymous, combined statistics from this app may be added to a large pool that Google uses to show businesses how they compare with others. Anything that could identify the app is removed first.
  • Technical support: Google support staff may look at our Analytics data and account when needed to fix a technical problem.
  • Account specialists: Google marketing and sales specialists may look at our Analytics data and account to suggest ways to set it up better.

We have not turned on the setting that shares this data with Google to improve Google’s own products and services. These settings apply only to the anonymous usage statistics above, and only if you chose to share them. If you say no, none of this applies to you.

5. Services that handle data for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore)
    Used for: sign-in and storing your synced copy.
    It sees: your Google account details and the synced study data, stored in Mumbai, India. Also, when you open Settings or the first-run sign-in link, the app downloads Google’s sign-in software from Google’s servers, which shows Google your internet address even if you never sign in.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (only if you agree to usage statistics)
    Used for: counting screens and features used.
    It sees: the anonymous events and device details listed in section 4. Google’s support and account specialists, and its anonymous benchmarking pool, may also see this data as described there.
  • Google Fonts
    Used for: loading the app’s fonts.
    It sees: your IP address and device type when fonts load.
  • Google Play (if you install from Play)
    Used for: installing the app and, if offered, paying for a plan.
    It sees: your payment details. We receive only whether a plan is active.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising trackers in the app.

6. How long we keep your data

  • On your phone: until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Online copy: until you delete it. If you stop using the app, it stays until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Usage statistics (only if you agreed): up to 2 months at Google, after which they are removed automatically.

7. Deleting your data and account

In the app: open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete my cloud copy and account. This removes everything we hold online for your account and deletes the account itself. Data on your phone is not touched; clear it from your phone’s settings or by uninstalling the app.

By email: if you cannot reach the app, write to rudraprasad734@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete your online data within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or erase it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by signing out and deleting your online copy. The in-app Download backup button gives you a copy of your data at any time.

For complaints, write to our grievance contact: Rudra Prasad Sharma (grievance contact), rudraprasad734@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database is set up so each signed-in user can reach only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we also recommend keeping an occasional backup file using the in-app button.

10. Who the app is for

The app is meant for adults preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date, and ask for your consent again where the law requires.

UPSC Companion is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Union Public Service Commission.
