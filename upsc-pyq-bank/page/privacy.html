Privacy Policy

CSE Companion · Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short

  • Without signing in, everything you enter stays on your own phone. We never receive it.
  • If you sign in, a private copy of your study data is kept in your account so you can restore it on another phone.
  • We do not sell your data or show ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off unless you say yes.
  • You can delete your online data and account at any time.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the CSE Companion app (“the app”, “we”) handles your data. CSE Companion is a study planner, timer and question bank for exam preparation. For any question about your data, write to our privacy contact: support@csecompanion.in.

2. Data we handle

Kept on your phone only (no sign-in needed). Your profile (name, district, state, title), study plan, chapters completed, study sessions, notes, timer history, mock-test scores, revision ticks, reminders and app settings. This stays in the app’s storage on your phone and is not sent to us.

If you sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID. The study data above (except reminder settings) is then also stored in a private area that only your account can read and write.

Optional usage statistics. Only if you say yes: which screens and features are used, your phone type, operating system, language and app version, your approximate location (country, state or city, worked out from your internet address), and a random installation ID. We never collect your name, email, notes, plan or anything you type as part of these statistics.

Technical data. When the app loads its content from our servers or our service providers, they see your internet address and device type, as any website does.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app and keep your data in step across your phones.
  • To let you restore your plan and progress if you change or lose your phone.
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the app (only if you agreed).
  • To keep the service secure.

We do not use your data for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share data only with service providers that work for us under our instructions: providers of cloud hosting and storage, sign-in, content delivery and fonts, and (if you agreed) analytics. They may process the data described in section 2 only to provide those services. Your synced data is stored on servers in India. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • On your phone: until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Online copy and account: until you delete them. We keep nothing about you afterwards, except where the law requires.
  • Usage statistics (if you agreed): removed automatically after up to 2 months.

To delete your account and online data: in the app, open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete my cloud copy and account. If you cannot open the app, use our account deletion page or email support@csecompanion.in from the address you signed in with. We delete your online data within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, and withdraw your consent at any time (sign out and delete your online copy). The in-app Download backup button gives you a copy of your data at any time. For requests or complaints write to support@csecompanion.in; we aim to reply within 7 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each signed-in user can reach only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we also suggest keeping an occasional backup file using the in-app button.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date, and ask for your consent again where the law requires.

10. Contact

Privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, and complaints: support@csecompanion.in.
