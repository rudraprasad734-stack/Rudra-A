Cancellation and Refund Policy

CSE Companion · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short

  • The first 14 days are free, with nothing charged, so you can try everything before paying.
  • Website payments are not refundable once access has been added, except in the cases in section 2 (a duplicate payment, a payment taken in error, or access we could not provide).
  • If you subscribed in the Android app, Google Play handles cancelling and refunds.

1. Free trial

Every feature is free for 14 days from the first time you open the app. We take no payment and ask for no card during the trial, so there is nothing to cancel.

2. Paid on the website (one-year pass)

One payment gives 12 months of access. It does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel and you are never charged again unless you choose to pay again. Access runs until the end date shown in Settings.

No refunds after access is added. You get a free 14-day trial to decide whether the app suits you, and the payment page shows the price and this policy before you pay. By paying you agree that the payment is for access to a study tool and is not refundable once access has been added. We do not promise any exam result, rank or selection, and a change of mind or an exam result is not a ground for a refund.

What we do refund. Write to support@csecompanion.in with your payment ID if:

  • you were charged twice for the same year by mistake,
  • money was taken but access was not added and we cannot fix it within 3 working days,
  • a payment was taken in error (for example the wrong amount), or
  • the app was unavailable to you for a long period because of a fault on our side.

Where one of these applies we refund the amount concerned to the original payment method. The year of access linked to a refunded payment ends when the refund is made. We start the refund within 3 working days of agreeing to it; banks and payment providers usually need 5 to 7 more working days to show it in your account.

This policy does not take away rights you have under Indian consumer law that cannot be waived.

3. Subscribed in the Android app (Google Play)

The subscription is billed by Google Play and renews every year until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play (Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CSE Companion), or with Manage subscription in the app. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy; we cannot refund Google Play payments ourselves.

4. Payment taken but access not shown

Access is normally added within a minute of paying. If money left your account and the app still asks you to pay, wait a few minutes and tap I have already paid. If it still does not unlock, email us with the payment ID. If a payment fails, the bank returns the money to you, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

5. Deleting your account

Deleting your account ends any one-year pass attached to it and does not trigger a refund. Please write to us before deleting if you want to keep the remaining time.

6. Contact

Questions or complaints: support@csecompanion.in. We answer within 3 working days.
